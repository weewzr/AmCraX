## Catalysts and conditions (Sec. 2.1, 2.3, p. 6-10)
- 10 wt% Ni and 2 wt% Ru on CeO2 and on Al2O3 (incipient wetness), plus three Ni-Ru/CeO2 bimetallics.
- 100 mg powder diluted with SiC to 0.45 cm3, 1/4" OD stainless-steel tube (wall 0.035"), 1 atm, 350-600 degC in 50 K steps (30 min each), total 23 mL/min, Ar:NH3 = 1.3:1, GHSV 3067 h^-1, F/W 13.8 L h^-1 g^-1. MS analysis (+/-5 %).

## Reported data
- Fig. 1 / text p. 10-11: Ru/CeO2 98 % vs Ni/CeO2 63 % conversion at 450 degC. Activity order Ru/CeO2 > bimetallics > Ru/Al2O3 > Ni/CeO2 >> Ni/Al2O3.
- Ru/CeO2 stable 36 % at 350 degC (p. 24, Fig. 2).
- TOF_H2 at 400 degC: Ru/CeO2 1.8, Ru/Al2O3 1.0, Ni/CeO2 0.03, Ni/Al2O3 0.01 s^-1 (p. 11).
- 100 h at 450 degC: Ni catalysts stable (Ni/Al2O3 activates), Ru/Al2O3 deactivates strongly (sintering), Ru/CeO2 less (Ru volatilisation) (Fig. 12, p. 26-27).

## What is missing for reproducible kinetic modelling
- No rate equation, no activation energy, no reaction orders, no pre-exponential factor for any catalyst. Only conversion curves (Fig. 1, values in text at 450 degC only) and TOFs.
- A pre-exponential factor can therefore NOT be derived from a data point (there is no published rate form or Ea to anchor it). Ni/CeO2 and Ru/CeO2 are NOT modelled in AmCraX.
- The essay statement "Ru/CeO2 98 % vs Ni/CeO2 63 % at 450 degC (1 atm, GHSV 3067 1/h, Ar:NH3 1.3:1)" is confirmed by the text (p. 10-11).
- The later paper of the same group (lucentini2021nirukinetic) gives a full LHHW law, but only for the Ni-Ru/CeO2 bimetallics.
