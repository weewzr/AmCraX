# 36 Purification (H4): PSA against a Pd-Ag membrane

Hypothesis H4 states that purification must balance purity against recovery, energy and cost. Section 28 compared purification configurations on the Phase 1 basis. Phase 2 compares pressure-swing adsorption (PSA) with a palladium-silver (Pd-Ag) membrane on the unified basis, with every input taken from a sourced range, and adds technology readiness levels (TRL). Both technologies share one plant, one boundary and one cost basis.

## 36.1 Inputs

The PSA H~2~ recovery is the decisive input and no measured value on cracked ammonia exists; the only published purity-recovery front for this gas composition is a simulation (Abdoos et al., 2025; Section 36.4). For industrial grade (99.9 %, 30 bar) the cases are 0.5715 (interpolated from Yáñez et al., 2020, for a different feed), 0.7516 (mass-balance assumption) and 0.85 (the AmCraX assumption); for ISO 14687:2019 grade D at 350 bar (International Organization for Standardization [ISO], 2019) they are 0.555, 0.7513 and 0.81 (Mehr et al., 2026, low confidence). Membrane inputs: cost 7,473 USD/m^2^ (6,800 EUR at 1.099 USD/EUR), 10,000 and 15,000 USD/m^2^; life 5, 3 and 2 years; flux multiplier 0.81 and 1.0; recovery 0.86, 0.90 and 0.905. Electricity runs from 0.084 to 0.2219 USD/kWh around the reference 0.1402.

**Membrane selectivity.** Cechetto et al. (2021) report an ideal H~2~/N~2~ selectivity for a new Pd-Ag membrane measured with single gases. The paper is internally inconsistent: the printed permeances give 40,541, while the text states more than 400,000, and the dead-end value gives 500,000. All three are carried: 40,541 (low), 400,000 (central) and 500,000 (high). The grade D requirement is 12,919 (13,100 in the worst-area case) and the industrial requirement 3,871, so the low value gives a margin of 3.1 times for grade D and 10.5 times for industrial grade. These are ideal single-gas values for a new membrane from one group, measured at 450 °C (the model runs at 400 °C), with trace NH~3~ only; no mixed-gas or aged value exists. Membrane scientists at the National University of Singapore (Zhang Sui and Zhao Dan) were identified, but none of their verified publications covers cracked-ammonia purification; nothing quantitative is used and no result is attributed to them.

## 36.3 Energy balance and purity cost (H4-e, H4-b)

The tail gas covers the furnace demand of 2,293 kW at a recovery of 0.7651 for either technology. Above it, the plant needs supplementary fuel: PSA at 0.85 leaves a 773 kW deficit and the membrane at 0.90 imports 4.61 kWh/kg. Below it, the lost hydrogen has no use in this flowsheet and surplus heat is not credited. That unrecovered hydrogen is burned in the furnace restates the flowsheet's tail-gas routing, an assumption built into the model, so it cannot support row H4-e by itself. The computed finding is the threshold: sourced and assumed PSA recoveries (0.555 to 0.85) lie on both sides of 0.765, so whether the lost hydrogen does useful work is undetermined, and row H4-e is INCONCLUSIVE. It was SUPPORTED before the independent review (Section 31.4). With rows H4-a, H4-b and H4-e all INCONCLUSIVE, component H4-B and hypothesis H4 are {{VERDICT:H4}}.

The cost order raw < industrial < grade D holds in every sourced combination and at every electricity price (3.784 / 3.984 / 4.237 USD/kg at 0.1402 USD/kWh). But raw < industrial is structural, most of the grade D increment is the 350 bar delivery, and the purity-only increment at equal 30 bar delivery is small: +0.017 USD/kg for the membrane polishing bed (proxy cost) and +0.002 (mass-balance relation) to +0.166 USD/kg (Yáñez points) for PSA; on the simulated front of Abdoos et al. (2025) it is +{{VAL:purification/phase2_comparison.json#abdoos2025_sourced_case/equal_delivery_increment/abdoos2025_table8/increment|.3f}} USD/kg, but three points of surrogate scatter reverse its sign (Section 36.4). Row H4-b stays INCONCLUSIVE under the strict row rule. Row H4-a (a PSA purity-recovery trade-off) is INCONCLUSIVE because AmCraX computes no PSA purity-recovery relation. Two published relations show the direction: the measured slope of Yáñez et al. (2020; 75.3 % to 55.5 % recovery between 99.25 and 99.97 % purity) for a different feed, and the simulated front of Abdoos et al. (2025) for a dry, ammonia-free cracked-ammonia composition, whose model and breakthrough check cite Yáñez et al. (2020), so the two are not fully independent. A literature direction is not a computed result, so the row does not move. Row H4-c is CONTRADICTED: the published PSA point of Cho et al. (2026), 99.11 mol% H~2~ with about 8,900 ppm N~2~, meets neither grade. Row H4-d (membrane against PSA) is INCONCLUSIVE because the ranking reverses inside the sourced ranges.

## 36.4 A simulated PSA front for cracked-ammonia composition (Abdoos et al., 2025)

Abdoos et al. (2025), read in full on 24 November 2026, simulate a four-bed, eight-step zeolite 5A cycle (Aspen Adsorption with a neural-network surrogate and NSGA-II optimisation) on a dry, ammonia-free 75/25 H~2~/N~2~ feed at adsorption pressures of 7.9 to 11.6 bar. No cyclic PSA experiment is reported. Their optimal points (Table 8) are 99.03 % purity at 83.92 % recovery, 99.95 % at 81.92 % and 99.99 % at 77.44 %. The direction agrees with row H4-a: higher purity costs recovery. The reference plant's assumption of 0.85 at 99.96 % lies above this front. The earlier search-summary figures attributed to this paper were wrong: "99.99 %, 77 %, 5.2 MJ/kg" joins two different points, and "a four-layer bed at 35 bar, 99.97 % and 79 %" is the paper's citation of a syngas study. The paper's specific energy (6.4 to 8.76 MJ/kg) is feed compression from about 1 bar and is not used, because the AmCraX PSA is fed at 19.5 bar.

The front is carried as a labelled sourced case beside the existing cases (`abdoos2025_sourced_case`); it replaces no input, no factorial case and no row rule. ## 36.5 Limits

There is no adsorption-cycle PSA model and no measured PSA recovery on cracked ammonia (the only published front for this composition is a simulation on a dry, ammonia-free feed at 8 to 12 bar). Membrane housing, skid and heating CAPEX, TSA heat, PSA power, NH~3~ inhibition and membrane ageing are not modelled. The NH~3~ price was not varied in this comparison. The browser simulator (Section 39) has no membrane model.
