# 31 Changes since the 23 November 2026 release

This is version 2.0 of the report (Phase 2). Version 1.0, the release of 23 November 2026 (178 pages), is preserved unchanged in `docs/technical_report/releases/2026-11-23/` and under the repository tag `amcrax-research-expansion-2026-11-23`; the result files it quotes are preserved in `results/research_releases/2026-11-23/`. Sections 1 to 30 keep their structure. Sections 31 to 39 and 43 are new, Section 40 replaces the former Section 31 (evidence matrix), and Sections 41 and 42 replace the former Sections 32 and 33.

## 31.1 What changed and why

Phase 2 put every research output on one techno-economic basis (Section 32), replaced assumed catalyst prices by prices derived from metal content (Section 35), added threshold analyses for the hypotheses whose outcome depends on an unsourced input (Sections 33 to 36), added variance-based sensitivity and Bayesian calibration diagnostics (Section 37), and applied a stricter verdict methodology whose roles, components, aggregation rule and general row standard were fixed before the analysis (Section 40). After an independent review on 24 November 2026 several results and three row verdicts were corrected (Section 31.4). The changes below are the ones a reader of version 1.0 needs to know. Several of them go against the preceding essay; they are stated as found.

The verdict row is the one that matters most and the one most easily misread. Version 2.0 applies a different rule (methodology v2, `docs/research/verdict_methodology_v2.md`), which judges each row against its own primary claim and then combines rows into components and components into a hypothesis verdict. What was fixed in advance must be stated precisely: only the row roles, the components, the aggregation rule and the general row standard were committed before the Phase 2 analyses (commit f233509) and have not been edited since. The row-by-row coded decision rules were written after the results were known. They were then tested by an independent adversarial review, which moved three rows to INCONCLUSIVE (Section 31.4). Version 1.0 required every row of a hypothesis to be SUPPORTED. The v1 verdicts are shown next to the v2 verdicts in Section 40, so a reader can see that the change in label comes from the rule as well as from new evidence. By the written rule a MIXED component counts for nothing at hypothesis level.

## 31.3 Two findings against the essay

Two Phase 2 findings go against the preceding essay and are repeated wherever H1 and H3 are discussed. First, on the reference basis, power from exhaust-heated full cracking is about 1.3 % cheaper than direct ammonia power (Section 33); direct ammonia is cheaper only against the fired and purified cracking pathways, and only under the equal-efficiency assumption. Second, with heat integration, at equal conversion, derived central catalyst prices and the Ni reference at its AmCraX-modelled 594 °C (an extrapolated law), three of the four low-temperature catalysts are dearer than Ni/MgO beyond the model resolution; 2.5Ni0.5Ru/CeO~2~ is within resolution; without heat integration the two NiRu catalysts are slightly cheaper; and against the sourced Ni design temperatures (736 and 760 °C) every low-temperature design is cheaper (Section 35). The Phase 1 figure of a 17 % fired-duty reduction was mostly an artefact of unequal conversion. Cho et al. (2026) used the published TEA price of 542 USD/kg for Ru-K/CaO, which lies below the ruthenium metal value.

## 31.4 Corrections after independent review (24 November 2026)

Three independent reviews (an adversarial review of the verdicts, a check of every document against the result files, and a review of the models and code) were carried out on 24 November 2026, after version 2.0 was first built. Their accepted findings were applied before this build. Every changed stored number and verdict, with its old and new value, is recorded in `results/research/fix_pass_2026-11-24.json`; Tables 31.2 and 31.3 are generated from that file.

**Verdicts.** The adversarial review found that three row rules could not fail as coded, or tested only half of their sub-claim, and moved them from SUPPORTED to INCONCLUSIVE, each with its threshold stated as the finding (in the evidence matrix: {{ROW_VERDICT:H2-b}}, {{ROW_VERDICT:H3-d}}, {{ROW_VERDICT:H4-e}}). Row H4-e: the sub-claim restates the flowsheet's tail-gas routing; the computed threshold recovery is 0.765 and sourced recoveries (0.555 to 0.85) lie on both sides.
